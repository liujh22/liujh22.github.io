This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, page views, traffic
sources, browser and device information, and interactions with its content.
Analytics starts only after you accept analytics cookies. Advertising consent
remains disabled.

Google Analytics uses cookies named `_ga` and `_ga_*` to distinguish visits and
sessions. Information about your use of this site is sent to Google. See
[how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The `cc_cookie` cookie remembers your choice in this browser for up to 182 days.
You can reject analytics and still use the site. Open **Cookie preferences** in
the footer to change your choice at any time. Withdrawing consent removes this
site's Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop further collection.
This does not delete information already sent to Google.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads some resources from other providers.
Those providers may receive technical request information independently of the
optional analytics described here.
