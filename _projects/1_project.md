## Included Papers

- **Lighting and circadian cues shape locomotor strategies for balance and navigation in larval zebrafish** (bioRxiv, 2025)  
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.18.689084>
- **Tau load in select brainstem neurons predicts the severity and nature of balance deficits in the absence of cell death** (bioRxiv, 2024)  
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.10.14.618073>
- **Gravity-Dependent Animacy Perception in Zebrafish** (Research, 2022)  
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.34133/2022/9829016>

## Focus of This Project

- Quantifying how light and circadian timing reshape locomotor control.
- Linking cellular pathology in defined brainstem populations to specific balance phenotypes.
- Testing how gravity-sensitive perception contributes to social/animacy computation in zebrafish.

More details are available on the [publications page](/publications/).
