I presented **Lighting and circadian cues shape locomotor strategies for balance and navigation in larval zebrafish** in Cassyni's _Ecology and evolution seminars_ series.

The talk explores how lighting and circadian cues shape the swimming strategies that larval zebrafish use to maintain balance and navigate their environment.

The related paper is now published in _Proceedings of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences_.
